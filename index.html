About

I'm a second-year Engineering Systems and Computing student at the University of Guelph, in the co-op stream. I like building both hardware and software: custom PCBs and firmware at the bench, and JavaScript and React on the web. Right now I'm working on my first full game.

Most of what I know came from making something small and then fixing whatever broke. That meant hackathons, two years in the STEMing UP program, lots of p5.js experiments, and teaching Python to other students. I care about making things that work for everyone, especially the people who often get left out when something is designed.
